About Coach Elena Vance

Master Fitness Specialist certified in Female Exercise Science, Biomechanics, and Precision Nutrition.

My Coaching Philosophy

Fitness shouldn't feel like a punishment or an endless cycle of extreme dieting. My mission is to give women the tools, education, and confidence to build athletic strength and body symmetry that lasts for life.

Over the past 12 years, I've mentored over 1,200 women ranging from post-partum mothers to competitive athletes, helping them drop body fat while retaining high energy and food freedom.
