Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your fitness coaching journey.

Do I need access to a commercial gym?
▼

No! Programs are fully customized to your equipment setup. Whether you train at a commercial gym or at home with dumbbells, we tailor your plan accordingly.

How are my workouts updated as I progress?
▼

During bi-weekly or weekly check-ins, we analyze your biofeedback, weight logs, and progress pictures to make continuous adjustments to your training volume and macros.

Will lifting weights make me bulky?
▼

Absolutely not! Females have a fraction of the testosterone required to build excessive bulk. Progressive weight training creates the toned, firm, hourglass shape most women desire.

How soon can I expect visible results?
▼

Most clients notice increased energy and initial body composition changes within 14 days, with significant physical transformations occurring between weeks 8 and 12.
